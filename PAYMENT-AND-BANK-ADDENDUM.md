----- Payment & Banking Addendum — Payment Verification, CBCS Review & License Activation Procedure -----

This Payment & Banking Addendum supplements the commercial agreement, Order, or invoice between Licensor and Customer and sets out the unequivocal procedures and conditions under which commercial license rights, warranties, indemnities, and support obligations (the "Commercial Terms") will become effective.

1. Activation Conditions (Conditions Precedent)

The Parties expressly agree that the Commercial Terms shall become binding and effective only when ALL of the following Activation Conditions have been satisfied (the "Activation Conditions"):

a) Execution: Licensor and Customer have executed a signed commercial agreement, Order, or equivalent written acceptance referencing the applicable Fees, Services, and the relevant invoice number(s).

b) Payment Cleared: Customer has caused full payment of the invoiced Fees to be received and irrevocably cleared in Licensor's nominated bank account or released from an agreed escrow agent. "Cleared" means funds are irrevocably available to Licensor and are not subject to recall, hold, chargeback, or reversal by any bank or intermediary.

c) Regulatory Confirmation (CBCS): Where the transfer or receipt of funds is subject to review, control, or approval by the Central Bank of Curaçao and St. Maarten ("CBCS") or by the Customer’s banking institution in connection with CBCS procedures, Customer must provide documented evidence of the required CBCS or banking confirmation. Evidence may be a bank-supplied CBCS reference number, a bank confirmation letter referencing CBCS clearance, or a CBCS-issued approval reference. If CBCS review is not required for the specific transaction, Customer shall provide a signed statement confirming that CBCS approval is not required.

d) Proof of Payment & KYC Documents: Customer shall provide Licensor with the following documents for each invoice or payment:
   - Invoice number(s) and corresponding Purchase Order (if applicable);
   - Bank payment confirmation showing sender, beneficiary, amount, date, and transaction reference (e.g., SWIFT MT103 / MT202 copy or bank remittance receipt);
   - KYC documents requested by Licensor’s bank (e.g., company registration certificate, tax ID, authorized signatory proof) where requested by Licensor or its bank.

The parties expressly agree that an instruction to pay or an unfulfilled payment instruction does not by itself satisfy the Payment Cleared condition. Activation requires actual cleared funds or escrow release as specified above.

2. Timing, Verification & Automatic Procedure

a) Submission: Customer must submit proof-of-payment documents and any CBCS/bank confirmation to Licensor’s billing contact at the billing email address specified on the invoice within forty‑eight (48) hours of initiating the payment.

b) Bank/Escrow Verification: Upon receipt, Licensor will seek bank confirmation (or escrow confirmation if escrow is used) that funds are cleared. Licensor will notify Customer within forty‑eight (48) hours after receiving valid proof-of-payment whether funds are cleared. If Licensor’s bank or escrow agent requires further documentation, Customer shall promptly provide it.

c) Activation Notice: Once Licensor confirms in writing (email to the billing contact and the licensee contact) that (i) funds have cleared or escrow has released the funds, and (ii) required CBCS/regulatory confirmation (if applicable) is on file, Licensor will issue a License Activation Notice. The Commercial Terms will be effective as of the date stated in the Activation Notice. Licensor will issue the Activation Notice within twenty‑four (24) hours of final verification where reasonably possible.

d) Delayed or Returned Payments: If funds are held, returned, or rejected by any banking intermediary, Licensor will promptly notify Customer and the Parties will work in good faith to resolve. If funds are not cleared within thirty (30) days of the invoice due date, Licensor may suspend delivery or terminate the Order and pursue remedies available under the commercial agreement.

5. Bank Fees, Taxes & Net Receipt

Customer shall pay or reimburse any taxes, duties, or charges applicable to the Fees (except taxes on Licensor’s net income). Customer shall also ensure that Licensor receives the full invoiced amount (net of any intermediary bank deductions). Customer shall bear its own banking fees and shall pay any intermediary or beneficiary bank charges that reduce the net remittance to Licensor. Where intermediary charges reduce the amount received by Licensor, Customer shall promptly reimburse Licensor to ensure Licensor receives the full invoiced sum.

6. Payment Confirmation and Evidence

Customer shall provide Licensor with payment confirmation evidence (e.g., MT103, MT202, bank remittance advice, or a bank statement entry) within forty‑eight (48) hours of payment initiation. Failure to provide timely proof may delay Activation. Customer authorizes its sending bank to provide copies of payment confirmations to Licensor upon request.

7. Disputes, Withholding & Partial Payments

If Customer intends to withhold payment for a bona fide dispute, Customer shall notify Licensor promptly with documentary evidence and shall pay any undisputed amounts. The Parties will resolve disputes in good faith. Withholding payment does not constitute satisfaction of the Activation Conditions for the withheld amount; the Commercial Terms will only be effective with respect to amounts for which Activation Conditions are satisfied.

8. Notification to Licensor's Bank and CBCS

Customer authorizes its sending bank to provide copies of payment confirmations (such as MT103 or MT202) to Licensor upon request and authorizes Licensor to present such confirmations (and any CBCS references) to Licensor’s bank or to CBCS for verification of clearance.

9. Failure to Provide Required Documentation

Licensor may refuse to accept payment that is not accompanied by the required proof-of-payment and regulatory confirmations. Licensor shall not be liable for delay or loss resulting from Customer’s failure to provide required documents or to obtain bank/CBCS confirmations in a timely manner.

10. Templates and Activation Notice

Licensor will maintain and provide upon request:
- an Example Invoice template that includes required remittance fields and CBCS reference placeholder;
- a License Activation Notice template (email and optionally a signed certificate) that Licensor will issue upon verification of cleared funds and compliance with Activation Conditions.

11. Incorporation & Governing Law

This Addendum is incorporated by reference into the Payment & Banking Addendum and the Commercial Terms between the Parties. Unless otherwise provided, this Addendum is governed by the laws specified in the underlying commercial agreement (default: laws of Curaçao).

12. Miscellaneous
