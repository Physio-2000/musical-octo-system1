Customer: [CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME] ("Customer").

2. License Grant
2.1 Subject to payment of Fees and compliance with this Agreement, Licensor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited license to use the Software for Customer's internal business operations in the environment and scope set out in the applicable Order.

4.1 Licensor shall deliver the Software and Documentation as specified in the Order. Customer shall perform any agreed acceptance testing within the acceptance period.

5. Limited Warranty
5.1 Licensor grants a limited warranty that the unmodified Software will materially conform to the Documentation for sixty (60) days from Delivery (the "Warranty Period"), provided the Software is used per Documentation in a supported environment.
5.2 The warranty excludes defects resulting from Customer modifications, unsupported environments, third-party components, or Customer-controlled infrastructure.

6. Remedies
6.1 Licensor's exclusive remedies for breach of the limited warranty are (a) repair or replace the non-conforming Software, (b) provide a workaround, or (c) refund Fees paid for the affected Software component.

7. Limitation of Liability
7.1 Except for willful misconduct or gross negligence, Licensor's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed USD 50,000.00.
7.2 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including lost profits or business interruption, except to the extent mandatory law requires otherwise.

8. Indemnity
8.1 Licensor will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the unmodified Software infringes third-party intellectual property rights, subject to Customer's prompt notice and cooperation and Licensor's control of defense.

9. Support and Maintenance
9.1 Support obligations, if any, shall be defined in a separate Support Schedule.

10. Data Protection
10.1 Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection laws. Licensor shall process Customer data only as instructed in writing or in a separate Data Processing Addendum.

11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Curaçao. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Curaçao unless agreed otherwise in writing.

12. Term and Termination
12.1 Either Party may terminate for material breach if the other fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice. Surviving clauses include Limitation of Liability, Indemnity, Confidentiality, and Governing Law.

13. Miscellaneous
13.1 Entire agreement, amendment, assignment, severability, and counterparts clauses are standard.

SECTION D — Payment & Banking Addendum (Activation Conditions)

PAYMENT & BANKING ADDENDUM

This Addendum sets out the Activation Conditions required before commercial rights, warranties, indemnities, and support obligations become effective.

1. Activation Conditions
The commercial rights become effective only when ALL the following are satisfied:
- Execution: a signed Agreement or Order referencing Fees and Services.
- Payment Cleared: full payment received and irrevocably cleared in Licensor's nominated bank account or released from escrow.
- Regulatory Confirmation: where applicable, Customer provides bank-supplied CBCS reference or bank/CBCS confirmation; otherwise a signed statement that no CBCS approval is required.
- Proof of Payment: Customer supplies proof-of-payment (SWIFT MT103/MT202, remittance advice) including invoice number, sender, beneficiary, amount, date, and bank reference.

2. Bank Details (Remittance fields)
Beneficiary name: Mr Dale Roger Cannegieter
Account number: 32447009
Bank: MADURO AND CURIELS BANK N.V.
Branch: 925 LIO CAPRILES BANKING CENTER
SWIFT/BIC: MCBKCWCU

3. Timing & Verification
- Customer to provide proof-of-payment within 48 hours of initiating remittance.
- Licensor will verify with bank/escrow and notify Customer within 48 hours of verification.
- Licensor will issue a License Activation Notice within 24 hours of final verification. Activation is effective as of the date in the Notice.

4. Escrow (Optional)
If escrow is used, Parties shall execute a separate escrow agreement; escrow release conditions shall mirror Activation Conditions.

5. Bank Fees & Net Receipt
Customer must ensure Licensor receives full invoiced amount net of intermediary deductions and reimburse Licensor for any shortfall.

6. Disputes
Withholding payment for bona fide dispute requires notice; disputed amounts do not meet Activation Conditions.
